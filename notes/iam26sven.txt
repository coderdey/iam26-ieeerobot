At the Lab This Week — Test the Trained Policy

This is the big moment. Deploy your trained model on the physical arm:

lerobot-rollout \
  --strategy.type=base \
  --policy.path=outputs/train/pick-drop-50-act/checkpoints/last/pretrained_model \
  --robot.type=so100_follower \
  --robot.port=/dev/ttyACM1 \
  --robot.id=follower_left \
  --robot.cameras="{ wrist: {type: opencv, index_or_path: 2, width: 1280, height: 720, fps: 10}, top: {type: opencv, index_or_path: 0, width: 2560, height: 720, fps: 30} }" \
  --task="Pick object and place in bin" \
  --duration=30

What to expect: The arm will attempt the task autonomously. First attempts are often shaky — that's normal. Watch for:
- Does it move toward the object?
- Does it attempt to grasp?
- Does it move toward the bin?
- Where does it fail?

Training Tips for Next Round

If you retrain, try enabling image augmee robust to lighting changes (importantfor competition day):

lerobot-train \
  --dataset.repo_id=mithi/pick-drop-50 \
  --dataset.root=/home/cris/.cache/huggingface/lerobot/mithi/YOUR_NEW_DATASET_FOLDER \
  --policy.type=act \
  --output_dir=outputs/train/pick-drop-50-act-aug \
  --job_name=act_pick_drop_aug \
  --policy.device=cuda \
  --policy.push_to_hub=false \
  --wandb.enable=false \
  --dataset.image_transforms.enable=true

The --dataset.image_transforms.enable=trntrast, hue, and affine transforms during training — makes the policy more robust to the different lighting at the competition venue.
